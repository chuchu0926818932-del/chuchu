## Intent

Add the daily SNL production batch: thirty wholly new short-video plans, split evenly between career/business-only women's growth, non-advisory money values, and non-labeling parenting relationships.

## Goals

- Follow the verified Keep-style arc: real-life friction, acknowledged pressure, plain explanation, one to three small choices, reframe, and one keyword CTA.
- Treat all existing code, retired topics, foundational topics, and Desktop Markdown/XLSX files solely as exclusions; do not derive or extend their ideas.
- Produce date-named Markdown and a formatted, one-sheet XLSX export without modifying retired material.

## Constraints

- Women's-growth plans stay within workplace boundaries, progression/pay, career change, positioning, entrepreneurship, confidence, collaboration, or leadership.
- Money plans contain no investment, lending, credit, or financial-product recommendations.
- Parenting plans avoid labeling children or promising behavioral outcomes.
- Thirty titles, hooks, scenes, core viewpoints, and CTAs are mutually unique, and eight outer formulas rotate evenly.

## Approach

Use the established dated-module, registry, focused-test, exporter, workbook, verifier, and Desktop-dedupe flow. It is the only approach that retains the app's production metadata and validates both deliverables against the full exclusion set.
